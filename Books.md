### Suggested path
1. **Months 1–2:** Math foundations (§1) alongside classic ML (§2)
2. **Months 3–4:** Deep learning foundations (§3) + practical engineering (§4)
3. **Months 5–6:** Pick your specialty — NLP (§5), CV (§6), or RL (§7)
4. **Month 7:** Generative AI / LLMs (§8) — most relevant to current industry roles
5. **Month 8+:** MLOps (§9) for production skills, then theory (§10) as you go deeper
