### Suggested path
1. **Weeks 1–4:** §1–2 — math and programming foundations, if not already solid
2. **Weeks 5–8:** §3–4 — classic ML then deep learning core courses (CS229 → CS230/fast.ai)
3. **Weeks 9–12:** §5–6 — specialize in NLP or computer vision based on your target role
4. **Weeks 13–14:** §7 — reinforcement learning, if relevant to your specialization
5. **Weeks 15–17:** §8 — LLMs and generative AI, the most in-demand current specialization
6. **Weeks 18–19:** §9 — MLOps, to round out production-readiness
7. **Ongoing throughout:** §10–11 for supplementary learning and §12 once you want to go deeper into a specific advanced topic
