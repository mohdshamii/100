### Suggested path
1. **Start:** §1–3 (tabular, image, text basics) — get comfortable with the ML workflow end-to-end
2. **Build:** §4–6 (Kaggle competitions, time series, audio) — practice real-world messiness
3. **Level up:** §7–8 (large-scale vision and NLP/pretraining corpora) — understand what powers modern models
4. **Specialize:** §9–12 (multimodal, RLHF/alignment, RL environments, recommenders) — pick based on your target role
