100-Day preparation plan

Systematic daily routine and phase progression

A rigorous, battle-tested structure combining theoretical reading, paper dissection, hands-on implementation, and interview preparation across 100 dedicated days.

Phase 1 · Days 01–20 Mathematical & Classical Foundations

Linear algebra (eigenvalues, SVD), multivariate calculus, probability distributions, followed by NumPy/Pandas pipelines and foundational regression/classification algorithms.

Books §1–2 Papers §1 Datasets §1 Projects §1
Phase 2 · Days 21–40 Deep Learning & Computer Vision

Multi-layer perceptrons, backpropagation derivations, optimization (SGD, Adam), CNN architectures (AlexNet, VGG, ResNet), and object detection with PyTorch.

Books §3, §6 Papers §2, §3 Datasets §2 Projects §3
Phase 3 · Days 41–60 Sequence Modeling & Transformers

From RNNs and LSTMs to Attention mechanisms. Deep study of "Attention Is All You Need", BERT, GPT-2/3, ViT, and building self-attention from scratch.

Books §5 Papers §4, §5 Tools §3 Projects §5
Phase 4 · Days 61–80 Generative AI, LLMs & Fine-Tuning

Diffusion models (DDPM, Latent Diffusion), Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (LoRA, QLoRA), RLHF, DPO, and building production RAG pipelines with vector databases.

Books §8 Papers §6, §10 Datasets §10 Projects §8
Phase 5 · Days 81–100 Production MLOps & Interview Mastery

Model serving with FastAPI and vLLM, Dockerization, monitoring, system design for recommendation/search engines, and intensive interview Q&A drills.

How to use this repository

Direct guidance from the curation files

Principles and practical advice assembled directly from the notes, suggested-path sections, and instructions written into the repository.

Books.md

Start with math and classic ML

The suggested path opens with Section 1 (Math & Stats Foundations) alongside Section 2 (Classic Machine Learning) across months 1–2, then Sections 3–4 (deep learning foundations and practical engineering) in months 3–4.

Books.md

Pick a specialty, then go current

Months 5–6 are for choosing NLP (§5), computer vision (§6), or reinforcement learning (§7). Month 7 moves to generative AI and LLMs (§8), which the file flags as most relevant to current industry roles, before MLOps (§9) and theory (§10) from month 8 onward.

ResearchPaper.md

Read chronologically through the fundamentals

Weeks 1–3 cover Sections 1–3: classic foundations, CNN architectures, and the RNN/LSTM era — establishing the historical build-up to modern deep learning.

ResearchPaper.md

Prioritize transformers and LLMs

Weeks 4–6 cover Sections 4–5 (attention, transformers, and large language models), which the file calls out as most relevant for AI/ML engineering roles today, before generative models, RL, self-supervision, and multimodal safety work.

Library Files

Links favor free, permanent sources

Books.md links to author official free versions or open textbooks where one exists; ResearchPaper.md links mostly to direct arXiv PDFs, with older classics linking to their original academic proceedings.

Library Files

Fallback when a link breaks

Each file gives the same instruction: search the title and author (Books.md) or the title on Google Scholar (ResearchPaper.md) rather than assuming the resource is unavailable.

Open source and community driven

The repository is actively maintained on GitHub. If you find a broken link, wish to propose an additional resource, or suggest curriculum enhancements, contribute directly via issues and pull requests.
