### Suggested use
1. **Don't try to follow everything at once.** Pick 2–3 newsletters (§4), 1–2 podcasts (§8), and a handful of people (§6–7) to start — that alone will keep you current.
2. **Use aggregators (§11) actively, not passively** — when you finish a paper from `ResearchPaper.md`, look it up on Connected Papers or Semantic Scholar to see what cites it and what it builds on.
3. **Rotate in company engineering blogs (§2)** when you're building projects from `Projects.md` — they're the best source for "how this is actually done in production" rather than research-only framing.
4. **Check research lab blogs (§1) weekly**, since that's where model/paper releases are announced first, often before wider newsletter coverage.
5. **Read policy/safety sources (§10) periodically**, even if your role is purely technical — understanding the alignment and safety landscape is increasingly expected of AI/ML engineers.
