### Suggested path
1. **Weeks 1–2:** §1–2 — get fluent in the data/numerics stack and classic ML libraries before touching deep learning
2. **Weeks 3–5:** §3 — pick one deep learning framework (PyTorch is the most widely used in research and most job postings) and go deep
3. **Weeks 6–8:** §4–5 — NLP/LLM tooling and computer vision libraries, based on your specialization
4. **Weeks 9–10:** §6–7 — experiment tracking and data pipelines, so your projects are reproducible, not just notebooks
5. **Weeks 11–12:** §8–9 — vector databases and model serving, the backbone of RAG and production LLM apps
6. **Weeks 13–14:** §10–11 — distributed training and MLOps infrastructure, for scaling beyond a single machine
7. **Ongoing:** §12 — pick up demo/dev tools as needed when building portfolio projects
